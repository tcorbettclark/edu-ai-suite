# Example credits — photographs and text

## Photographs

The three photographs are from the
[Cirrus Cumulus Stratus Nimbus (CCSN) database](https://doi.org/10.7910/DVN/CADDPD)
(Zhang et al., 2018), released under CC0 1.0. The Hub mirror used here is
[aduuuuuu/CCSN](https://huggingface.co/datasets/aduuuuuu/CCSN) (MIT).

- `cirrus.jpg` — CCSN row 919, class Ci (cirrus)
- `volutus.jpg` — CCSN row 2001, class Sc (stratocumulus)
- `low_cloud.jpg` — CCSN row 1545, class Cu (cumulus)

Please cite:

> Zhang, J. L., Liu, P., Zhang, F., & Song, Q. Q. (2018). CloudNet: Ground-based
> cloud classification with deep convolutional neural network. Geophysical
> Research Letters, 45, 8665–8672. https://doi.org/10.1029/2018GL077787

## Text

`happy_prince.txt` is Oscar Wilde's *The Happy Prince and Other Tales* (1910), from
[Project Gutenberg eBook #902](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/902). The text is in
the public domain in the United States. The file keeps Project Gutenberg's header and
footer; the language-model scripts strip them with `clean_text`.

`dorian_gray.txt` is Oscar Wilde's *The Picture of Dorian Gray* (1891), from
[Project Gutenberg eBook #174](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/174). The text is in
the public domain in the United States. The file keeps Project Gutenberg's header and
footer; the language-model scripts strip them with `clean_text`. SHA-256 of the
downloaded file: `0c9a2cbf188ca6534c60381b764ac37bf4594b16971aea5cc8263e83b9ce9a05`.

`tiny_stories.txt` is a 500-story subset of
[TinyStories](https://huggingface.co/datasets/roneneldan/TinyStories) (Eldan & Li,
2023), released under the
[Community Data License Agreement – Sharing, Version 1.0](https://cdla.dev/sharing-1-0/)
(CDLA-Sharing-1.0). TinyStories contains synthetic short stories generated by GPT-3.5
and GPT-4 using a small vocabulary. The subset uses the first 500 complete stories of
`TinyStories-train.txt`. The `<|endoftext|>` separators are removed and the stories are
joined by a blank line. SHA-256:
`877113b5ba3ed7b3abc20f22a1a2c0f786d88aa56bc93921eef74a19b63177fe`.

Please cite:

> Eldan, R., & Li, Y. (2023). TinyStories: How Small Can Language Models Be and Still
> Speak Coherent English? arXiv:2305.07759.
